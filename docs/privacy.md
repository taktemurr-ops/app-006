# Makro 360 — privacy and export notes

All entries remain in the device's platform-protected secure storage. No registration or advertising analytics SDK is included in this development build. The user can copy a full JSON export to the system clipboard or paste a previous export to restore their records. **Clipboard content is not encrypted** and may be visible to other clipboard-aware apps; advise users not to export on shared devices. Entries are estimates entered by the user, not medical guidance.

The current release does not configure Apple/Google in-app purchase products, live advertisements, signing or store privacy disclosures. No publication claim is made before these items, localization review and device tests are verified.
